# VISTA O MOMENTO DE NALOGES — Visual system

## Direction

Moda urbana com linguagem editorial e energia de pôster independente. A interface deve parecer uma publicação de moda que também vende: contexto e desejo vêm antes do inventário, mas a compra permanece a um gesto de distância.

## Palette

- **Black** `#0D090B`: canvas, navigation, footer, and hard contrast.
- **Pink** `#FF4FA3`: calls to action, labels, active states, and brand energy.
- **Red** `#EF263F`: editorial emphasis, section shifts, and secondary actions.
- **Light pink** `#FFD6E7`: primary type and product information.
- **Muted pink** `#C58DA2`: supporting copy only.

## Typography

- **Syne** for large, compressed, expressive display headlines.
- **Manrope** for readable UI and product names.
- **DM Mono** for labels, prices, edition codes, and utility text.

## Composition

The landing view uses a split campaign composition rather than a generic centered hero: the oversized message creates the thesis and the image carries the look. Catalog pages use a restrained four-column grid with visible filter and search controls. Red sections are used as full-bleed editorial interruptions, not decoration.

## Interaction

The primary action is lime and uses a small directional arrow. Product images scale subtly on hover, favorites remain local and immediate, and the bag slides from the edge with quantity controls. Motion is short and directional, preserving the feeling of a printed editorial system becoming interactive.

## Responsive rules

At narrow widths, navigation simplifies to the wordmark and actions, campaign columns stack, the product grid becomes two columns, and the bag retains full-width usability. The lime action bar remains visible on product cards for touch users.
